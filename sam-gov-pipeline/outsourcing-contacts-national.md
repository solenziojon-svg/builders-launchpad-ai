## Subcontracting reality check
Most NCA/DoD grounds contracts are SDVOSB or HUBZone set-asides with 51% self-performance clauses (FAR 852.219-75). Non-SDVOSB primes like CJS can only sub on total-small-business or unrestricted solicitations, or on the non-set-aside portion. Prioritize Custom Lawn Service, Davey, Pride Industries, and Elegant/ATK-type locals for those.
